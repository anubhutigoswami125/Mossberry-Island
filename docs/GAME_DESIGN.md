# Game Design: Mossberry Island

## Pillars
1. A place, not a checklist: the island should feel lived in without a constant objective.
2. Small things matter: remembered kindness and details in familiar places should count.
3. Gentle agency: exploration, collecting, home-making, and companionship are valid play styles.
4. Discover the story through people, objects, and places.
5. Every visible control must work; AI is optional and never authoritative.

## First vertical slice
The North Meadow connects the player's cottage, the Old Oak, Moonpond, and a winding path. The player can move, inspect nearby objects, collect a wild berry, button mushroom, and bluebell, and meet Rowan Thistle. One real second advances one in-game minute; an in-game day lasts 24 real minutes. The clock begins at 8:00 AM.

## Controls
- WASD or arrow keys: walk.
- E: interact with the closest object in range.
- Click: interact with a nearby point of interest.
- Escape: close dialogue.

## Prototype boundaries
The scene uses original code-drawn prototype art. Separate interiors, touch controls, furniture placement, farming, cooking, shops, full calendar, audio, and AI are not yet implemented.
