# Asset Guide

## Current prototype art
The meadow, cottage, pond, trees, Rowan, flowers, berry, and mushroom are drawn with Phaser Graphics primitives in src/main.ts. They are original procedural prototype art, not final illustration or sprite assets. No third-party game art is bundled.

## Future asset rules
For each asset record its stable filename and ID, purpose and dimensions, format and animation layout, source URL and creator, exact license and attribution, modifications, and usage. Prefer original art or assets with explicit redistribution permission. Publicly viewable does not mean reusable. Keep a consistent perspective and define sprite scale before commissioning artwork.
