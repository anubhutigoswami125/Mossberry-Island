# World Bible

## Premise
Mossberry Island is a small green island where old footpaths predate the village and certain stones are said to hum at dawn. It is a place of quiet rituals, imperfect cottages, seasonal plants, and neighbours who know the landscape by heart.

## Established facts
- The first playable location is the North Meadow.
- The player has recently arrived and has a cottage to make their own.
- The Old Oak stands in the meadow; Rowan tends plants and herbs nearby.
- Moonpond lies east of the cottage path.
- The old paths existed before the cottages.
- Rowan has heard the old stones hum, a sound that can be mistaken for bees.
- The island mystery unfolds gradually and is not resolved in the opening scene.

## Rowan Thistle
- Role: local herbalist and caretaker of small, stubborn plants.
- Voice: observant, warm but not effusive; uses sensory details and gentle dry humour.
- Knows: plant lore, old footpaths, seasonal habits, stories about humming stones.
- Does not know the full explanation for the island's quiet or the player's larger role.
- First meeting: welcomes the player and suggests noticing small things.
- Likes wild berries and practical kindness.
- Relationship arc: neighbourly first connection that grows through helping restore overlooked places.

## Narrative guardrails
Do not contradict established facts without updating this document. Do not make residents omniscient or force the main mystery into every conversation. Avoid generic prophecy language; prefer specific local details and human-scale stakes.
